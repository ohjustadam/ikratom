Two changes this week make the toolbelt faster and open it up to everyone — and we rounded up everything else that's landed lately.

## 🌎 Every campaign is now actionable — wherever you live

Until now, a campaign for another state would stop you at the door. That's gone. **Every active campaign is now something you can act on**, regardless of the state on your profile (or whether you've set one at all).

- **Your own fights still come first.** Campaigns in your state float to the top, and your own reps are still a single click away.
- **Out-of-state? Add your voice anyway.** Plenty of fights need backup from beyond state lines. When you open a campaign somewhere you don't live, you pick exactly which officials to contact — no spraying a whole legislature, just the message you mean to send. We're upfront that lawmakers weigh constituent mail most, so lead with *why it matters to you*.
- **Browse it all.** The campaigns page now shows everything by default, with search and filters to zero in on a state, a scope (federal / state / city), or a stance.

## 📧 Connect your email once → one-click everywhere

The single biggest speed-up is connecting your email. Do it once and **any** campaign sends in a single click — each message from **your** address, each landing in your Sent folder, exactly what legislators actually read.

- Works with **Gmail** and **Outlook**, with the narrowest possible permission — send-only. We never read your inbox, contacts, or anything else, and we don't store your messages.
- No Gmail or Outlook? You can still send from your own email app on any campaign — nothing's locked behind connecting.
- There's now a reminder right on the campaigns page so it's easy to set up and get back to taking action.

## ✨ Recently shipped

A lot has landed in the last couple of weeks:

- **🔊 Listen to anything.** Bills, state briefings, and your daily brief can now be read aloud — tap **Listen** and take it in on the go.
- **🗳️ Elections, primaries & a real calendar.** A Community Calendar pulls in your state's primary, runoff, and general election dates (plus registration deadlines), town halls, hearings, and bill actions — and you can subscribe to it in your own calendar app.
- **⏰ Voting reminders.** Opt-in nudges at T-30, T-7, and T-1 days before each election in your state, with quiet hours respected.
- **✊ Communities that beat a ban.** The banned-tracker now highlights the places that fought a local ban and won — proof it can be done.
- **🔔 Notification settings + Do Not Disturb.** Fine-grained control over what pings you, and when.
- **📍 Set your state.** A two-minute setup that tailors the whole toolbelt to where you live.
- **📲 Install it as an app.** iKratom installs to your home screen on iPhone, Android, and Windows.
- **✍️ Personalize with your story.** Turn a template into your authentic voice in one tap before you send.

## 🔭 What we're working on

A peek at what's coming, in rough order:

- **More ways to listen** — additional voices and finer controls for the read-aloud features.
- **A friendlier front door** — a redesigned home and a personal "My District" view that answers *"what's my one thing to do today?"* the moment you land.
- **Bill watchlists** — track the bills you care about and get pinged when they move.
- **More local coverage** — filling in city and county officials so local campaigns reach the right desks.

We build in the open — every change here links back to the code. Thanks for being part of it. 💚
