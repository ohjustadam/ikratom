Here's what changed since mid-September. Short version: more of the officials who vote on kratom near you are now one click away, your news alerts work again, and pages load faster.

## 🏛 More local officials to contact

- **Seven new county boards are covered**, many of them near places with an upcoming kratom hearing: Albany, Saratoga, Schenectady, Warren and Washington Counties in New York, DuPage County in Illinois, and Burleigh County in North Dakota.
- **We re-checked more than 30 city and county rosters** against each government's own website, including Atlanta, Boston, Denver, Phoenix, Raleigh and Washington, DC. Officials who have left office were removed and their replacements added, so your letters go to the people serving now.
- **Places with no county government are handled honestly.** Connecticut, Rhode Island and most Massachusetts counties have no county board. Your city or town council represents you there instead.

## 📰 News and alerts

- **News notifications are working again.** For a stretch, news alerts were quietly sending nothing. That's fixed, and there's now a country-wide news digest too.
- **The freshest stories come first.** New articles get checked and summarized before older ones.

## 📅 Hearings and meetings

- **Only official sources can put a meeting on the calendar**: a government's own website or its official agenda system. That keeps rumors and reposts out.
- **Confirmed hearings show up sooner**, because a hearing verified against its official agenda can now publish without waiting in a queue.

## ✉️ Email

- **Sign-up and password-reset emails always get through**, even on busy days, because they have their own reserved capacity.
- **Digest emails deliver reliably** now that every one includes an unsubscribe link, which email providers require.

## ⚡ Faster pages

- Pages for signed-out visitors are served from a fast cache, and links load when you're about to click them rather than all at once. The site should feel quicker, especially on phones.
