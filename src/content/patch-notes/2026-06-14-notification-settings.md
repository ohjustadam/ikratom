We just handed you the volume knob. No more all-or-nothing notifications — pick exactly what reaches you, and when.

## 🎚️ Notify me about exactly what I care about

Open **Settings → Notifications** and toggle each category on or off:

- **Bills** — status changes on legislation you're tracking
- **News** — kratom policy coverage in your state
- **Your reps** — when we add or update officials near you
- **Meetings** — hearings and local votes
- **Community** — forum replies and the Lounge
- **Announcements** — product updates like this one

## 🌙 Do Not Disturb

Set **quiet hours** (and your time zone) so iKratom never buzzes you in the middle of the night. Flip DND on and we'll hold notifications until you're awake.

Tune it once, and we'll only reach out the way you want. 👉 [Open your notifications](/notifications)
