A real news reader, better de-duplication, and natural-sounding audio.

## ✨ New features

- **Read the news in-app.** A proper reader at /news/[id] instead of bouncing you to a publisher, plus a rebuilt index with dual dates, view toggles and pagination. ([d2e889c](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/d2e889ceefbc8e79ab719a5da40de3060d2c8a20), [40fe1db](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/40fe1db20e0f7c6f14ee8a37a6bbb77bbc7c24a0))
- **Audio that sounds like a person.** Neural text-to-speech replaces the robotic voice for listening on the move. ([c96af86](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/c96af867ecf6ef038d07e41af622c928bfcc3083))
- **The same story stops appearing five times.** Syndicated coverage is collapsed hourly into one entry. ([a8b405f](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/a8b405fe1d3ba5832b53b92e1bf92c9c9de7b45d), [5f5042e](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/5f5042e601237a5658e36496973925a7ae2cb2c1))
- **Stale push subscriptions clean themselves up,** so notifications stop going to devices that no longer exist. ([33ed4c6](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/33ed4c63ace52fb09cc103a7c0f63e810c680d53))

## 🛠 Under the hood

Reliability, security and maintenance work landed this period too. Nothing you need to do — the toolbelt just gets sturdier.
