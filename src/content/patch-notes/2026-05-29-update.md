A real news reader, better de-duplication, and natural-sounding audio.

## ✨ New features

- **Read the news in-app.** A proper reader instead of bouncing you to a publisher, plus a rebuilt index with dual dates, view toggles and pagination.
- **Audio that sounds like a person.** Neural text-to-speech replaces the robotic voice for listening on the move.
- **The same story stops appearing five times.** Syndicated coverage is collapsed hourly into one entry.
