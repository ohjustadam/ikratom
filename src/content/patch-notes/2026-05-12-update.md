## ✨ New features

- **Invite friends** with your own link, and share it on 11 platforms.
- **A simpler menu.** The top navigation is now four dropdowns, with a share button in the header.
- **Share buttons on every page.**
- **Install iKratom** on your phone from a new install prompt.
- **A new home page** that puts your next action first.
- **Per-state briefings** on what's happening where you live.
- **Call tracking** for the calls you make to officials.
- **A microphone meter and earbud tips** for calling from the app.
- **Local government meetings** are found automatically, and a new visual calendar shows them.
- **Alert cards always show their date**, including on your watch list.
