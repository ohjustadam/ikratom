A busy week for the toolbelt. Here's what's new for you:

## 🌎 Every campaign, actionable from anywhere

Campaigns no longer stop at the state line — you can act on any active campaign wherever you live. Your own state's fights still come first and your reps stay one click away; when you weigh in from out of state, you pick exactly which officials to contact. [Browse campaigns →](/campaigns)

## 📧 Connect your email once → one-click everywhere

Link your Gmail or Outlook once and any campaign sends in a single click — each message from your own address, with send-only permission (we never read your inbox). No account to link? You can still send from your own email app on any campaign.

## 🏛️ A rebuilt State HQ

Every state page now opens with **your local reps** — the legislators who answer to you — with one-click email, call, and briefing links. The daily state briefing (with one-tap **Listen**) is folded into the same page, so a state's whole picture lives in one place.

## 🗓️ Tune your daily brief

The daily brief now has **state and day selectors** — check any state's brief, or catch up on a previous day, instead of just today and your own.

## 🔊 Listen, don't just read

The read-aloud **Listen** button keeps spreading — state briefings now read aloud in the same on-device neural voice as the daily brief. Tap it and take it in on the go.

## 🛠️ Under the hood

A large reliability and security hardening pass landed this week, plus behind-the-scenes work to keep campaign data fresh and accurate. Nothing you have to do — the toolbelt just gets sturdier.

We build in the open. Thanks for being part of it. 💚
