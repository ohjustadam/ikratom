## 🎯 The big one — Threat matrix

Every active legislator scored and ranked into seven action tiers:

- 🚨 **Active opponents** — hostile + primary sponsor of restrictive legislation. Organize counter-pressure.
- ⚠ **Hostile decision-makers** — hostile + on a kratom-deciding committee. Block procedurally.
- 🎯 **Flippable targets** — neutral / unknown stance on a kratom committee, low donor conflicts. Highest conversion ROI.
- ⭐ **Champions** — pro-kratom legislators to reinforce.
- 🤝 **Sympathetic allies** — cosponsors and quiet supporters to upgrade.
- 📚 **Education targets** — neutral with no power. Build the relationship.

Composite scoring across **stance, sponsorships, committee leverage, donor industries, and STOCK Act personal trades**. Click any row for the full briefing. Live count: **70 active opponents across 18 states**.
