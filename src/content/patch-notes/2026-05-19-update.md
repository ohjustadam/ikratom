The Daily Advocate Brief arrives, plus committee-chair targeting and a coordination layer for advocates working the same bill.

## ✨ New features

- **Your day, on one page.** The Daily Advocate Brief pulls what moved overnight into a single read, opens with a plain-language summary of the day, and can arrive as a push so you don't have to go looking. ([dae8503](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/dae8503bb6d3d4c1128ecbb77d1cebad4b60367f), [86be9b8](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/86be9b818303c42b0605fd2e5786e9139d2b6018), [1186f63](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/1186f63de78a08240f04606ed1d7018dfeb8c596))
- **Aim at the person who can actually stop a bill.** Committee-chair targeting surfaces the chair holding your bill, and a momentum read estimates where it is heading rather than just where it has been. ([5e9f3e2](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/5e9f3e26d2e751e11e32a98624ef4c56ec54b6eb), [2864600](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/2864600deba8bcaf253ea614a5acffd9045ca2f0))
- **Work the same bill without stepping on each other.** A coordination layer, a shared activity log, and Story Bank matching so a personal story gets attached to the response it fits. ([1cf001d](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/1cf001dc97edc5f9fc2b1166908126f39b9b0af8), [6edcb73](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/6edcb731bc14b5462a3e5b18edc10f02d9d51c4f), [7069545](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/706954546e2f87f886e0a5ee85210bd6a796be4e))
- **Bills and research, linked both ways.** Open a bill and see the studies that bear on it; open a study and see the bills. ([093869d](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/093869d167bf82d4ed178bcc5d2342555a4798d8))
- **A one-tap action panel built for a phone.** /now puts the next useful thing in front of you when you have two minutes in a parking lot. ([e424614](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/e4246148e242a00b4817e13b06cf7af93fcb309a))
- **Copy is editable from any device,** so a typo on a live page is a 30-second fix, not a deploy. ([c309858](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/c30985820a32549a0a3aef57d8463defd2930f74))
- **The automation watches itself now** — a health dashboard over every scheduled job, and a support page for anyone who wants to help keep it running. ([a9b26b9](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/a9b26b93df344a27f75fc6e61c9230ff8ceffd1c), [f714fe2](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/f714fe251f2e1a54fa3e5610ed93f2e7f6c2d744))
