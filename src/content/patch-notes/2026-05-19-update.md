The Daily Advocate Brief arrives, plus committee-chair targeting and a coordination layer for advocates working the same bill.

## ✨ New features

- **Your day, on one page.** The Daily Advocate Brief pulls what moved overnight into a single read, opens with a plain-language summary of the day, and can arrive as a push so you don't have to go looking.
- **Aim at the person who can actually stop a bill.** Committee-chair targeting surfaces the chair holding your bill, and a momentum read estimates where it is heading rather than just where it has been.
- **Work the same bill without stepping on each other.** A coordination layer, a shared activity log, and Story Bank matching so a personal story gets attached to the response it fits.
- **Bills and research, linked both ways.** Open a bill and see the studies that bear on it; open a study and see the bills.
- **A one-tap action panel built for a phone.** /now puts the next useful thing in front of you when you have two minutes in a parking lot.

## 🐛 Fixes

- Placeholder records no longer show up as real citations.
