Big campaign sends now run in the background and survive a closed tab, every briefing can be listened to, and the 7-OH scheduling packet is live.

## ✨ New features

- **Send a big campaign without babysitting it.** Large sends now go through a durable queue that knows your mail provider's daily limit, paces itself under it, and picks up where it left off instead of starting over. You can watch it work — and close the tab without losing the batch. ([2cb363a](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/2cb363a32943bc2a853b96efaa77810aa80b735a), [f91b182](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/f91b1829a8526c9dfa909a1f10d922e6581e4ac7), [0da28f7](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/0da28f7d343729b2efcfc137dbf42c73c637ebef))
- **Correct your email provider if we guessed wrong.** We detect your provider to set the right sending limit. If the guess is off, you can now fix it yourself rather than hitting a wall mid-campaign. ([5664e02](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/5664e02f1817c3b12cc89f54568d7fc8e01cf84e))
- **Listen to any briefing.** Every briefing now has a Listen button with real playback controls — seek, skip, speed, and a choice of voices — reading a script written for the ear, so charts and citation tables don't get narrated as fragments. Useful on the drive to your capitol. ([6dcbe46](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/6dcbe4697ce2a84aef6f25d3374c196baabf7eda), [1652ce1](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/1652ce1479c4147b6888d5f467c50432d060736c), [81ae0cb](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/81ae0cb0383c2f9803b1745906851c5db0ce8374))
- **7-OH scheduling intel packet.** A full briefing on what the DEA's temporary Schedule I order actually covers as of August 26 — what is banned, what is not, and where the real drafting gaps are. The federal comment period has reopened, and the packet is built to be forwarded. ([5f3ccac](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/5f3ccac7267b6f3d9adffd9c26f8cc520bc376aa), [0fe4fc1](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/0fe4fc1d49f2ee503053e8460c56abf44e06821d))

## 🐛 Fixes

- **A paused send now resumes properly** — and tells you what moved when it does. ([683b845](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/683b845df073076f81b09c6cc1dbd6042b05e582))
- **Briefing dates read as dates again.** One briefing was showing its publication date as a full raw timestamp in the server's timezone instead of a plain date. ([1f5b025](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/1f5b02504bad10e456d21da687cab3bcdbc69bb0))
- **The "Copy share link" button on briefings works reliably,** and briefings shared to social now carry a proper description instead of an empty one. ([3289355](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/3289355eb74863e0ce740a430c375e8a02f36df0))
- **Nightly maintenance is steadier,** and every account is guaranteed a public handle. ([5f5921d](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/5f5921d5a3fbcfec83ff40d71833aa2b128febb5))

## 🛠 Under the hood

Reliability and security improvements plus behind-the-scenes maintenance landed this period. Nothing you need to do — the toolbelt just gets sturdier.
