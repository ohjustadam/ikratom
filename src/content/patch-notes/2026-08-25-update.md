Big campaign sends now run in the background and survive a closed tab, every briefing can be listened to, and the 7-OH scheduling packet is live.

## ✨ New features

- **Send a big campaign without babysitting it.** Large sends now go through a durable queue that knows your mail provider's daily limit, paces itself under it, and picks up where it left off instead of starting over. You can watch it work — and close the tab without losing the batch.
- **Correct your email provider if we guessed wrong.** We detect your provider to set the right sending limit. If the guess is off, you can now fix it yourself rather than hitting a wall mid-campaign.
- **Listen to any briefing.** Every briefing now has a Listen button with real playback controls — seek, skip, speed, and a choice of voices — reading a script written for the ear, so charts and citation tables don't get narrated as fragments. Useful on the drive to your capitol.
- **7-OH scheduling intel packet.** A full briefing on what the DEA's temporary Schedule I order actually covers as of August 26 — what is banned, what is not, and where the real drafting gaps are. The federal comment period has reopened, and the packet is built to be forwarded.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **A paused send now resumes properly** — and tells you what moved when it does.
- **Briefing dates read as dates again.** One briefing was showing its publication date as a full raw timestamp in the server's timezone instead of a plain date.
- **The "Copy share link" button on briefings works reliably,** and briefings shared to social now carry a proper description instead of an empty one.
- **Nightly maintenance is steadier,** and every account is guaranteed a public handle.

## 🛠 Under the hood

Reliability and security improvements plus behind-the-scenes maintenance landed this period. Nothing you need to do — the toolbelt just gets sturdier.
