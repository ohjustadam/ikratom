Here's what landed this week — and a 30-second thing that makes all of it work better for you.

## ✉️ Email any official — in one place, with AI help

Every "Email" button across iKratom — your legislators, bill pages, the directories, your matched reps — now opens the **same composer**: a ready-to-send letter that you can rewrite **in your own voice with one tap** (it uses your kratom story if you've added one), then send through Gmail, Outlook, or your default mail app.

- Officials with **no public email** (a lot of governors and attorneys general) no longer dead-end — the composer takes you straight to their official contact form.
- **Your street address is never put in any email.** Letters sign off with your name + city/state only. Privacy by default.

## 📍 Your LOCAL officials now match you — city *and* county/parish

If you live in an unincorporated area, a CDP, or a parish/county rather than an incorporated city, your **local council or commission** now shows up under **My District**. This was quietly broken for a lot of rural and unincorporated advocates — it's **fixed for everyone** now.

## 🏅 You now earn points for advocacy

Sending an email or making a call earns **advocacy points**, shown on your dashboard. It's the start of something bigger (see below).

## 🔒 Privacy & security, hardened

We closed several ways your identity could have leaked: **anonymous intel tips are now truly anonymous**, and profile + direct-message protections were tightened. Your anonymity is the whole point — we treat it that way.

## 👉 Do this today (30 seconds)

**Add your address** in your account, and **request your local officials** on your state page.

- Your address is used **only** to match you to your exact districts. It's **never shown to anyone and never included in an email.**
- Once it's set, contacting the specific people who represent you — from your city council up to your member of Congress — becomes genuinely one click.

## 🔭 What we're building next

- **iKratom Academy** — a free, cited, university-style course on kratom & 7-OH: the science, the law, and how to actually move a legislator. Learn at your own pace, earn points, and (soon) a **verified "power advocate" badge** you earn by passing — not by clicking through.
- More **one-click local action** as we widen local-official coverage.

Thank you for being here. Every message you send is on the record where it counts.
