Faster page loads, a fixed password-reset link, an updated privacy policy, and groundwork for the mobile app stores.

## ✨ New

- **Pages load faster.** Every page now paints instantly with a loading skeleton instead of a blank screen, and we trimmed the code your browser downloads on first visit. More speed work is rolling out this week.
- **App-store groundwork.** We prepped iKratom for Google Play and Apple App Store packaging — same site, soon installable from the stores. (The install-from-browser app at [/install](/install) already works today.)
- **Privacy policy refreshed.** We updated [/privacy](/privacy) to describe exactly what we use: first-party product analytics (inputs always masked, nothing sold or shared) and push-notification subscriptions you opt into. No third-party ad trackers — that hasn't changed.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **"Forgot password?" works again** — the link in the sign-in window pointed at a missing page. If you were locked out, [reset here](/forgot).
- **The daily data refresh is reliable again.** A background sync had been quietly failing for days; it's fixed, and the platform now pages its operator automatically if any sync goes silent.

## 🛠 Under the hood

The operator toolkit grew: every background pipeline can now be run with one click from inside the admin panel, so data refreshes never wait on a developer. Reliability and security hardening landed across the board — nothing you need to do, the toolbelt just gets sturdier.
