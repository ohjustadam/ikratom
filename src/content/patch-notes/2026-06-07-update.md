A big stretch of work landed this week — a brand-new front door, a far more accurate legal-status map, and a sweep of fixes to the parts you use most.

## ✨ New

- **A brand-new home page.** Visitors now land on a real front door: a live "what's happening right now" pulse, a state-by-state legal-status map, a candle in memory of Scot Rubi, and a faster way to get started.
- **The legal-status map now covers 37 states.** Each status was cross-referenced against the statute plus multiple independent sources and fact-checked before publishing — and we corrected Kansas (its new law schedules only 7-OH; the natural leaf stays legal). States we're still verifying stay grey, never a guess.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **One-click email actions are reliable again.** They no longer open a blank message or crash — they load your actual officials, every time.
- **Clean source links everywhere.** Alerts and the letters you send to officials now show a tidy, titled article link instead of a long Google-redirect URL.
- **Notifications load reliably again** — and we fixed the deeper bug behind it that had been quietly breaking one-click actions across the whole site.
- **Smarter alerts.** Alerts mislabeled to the wrong state are now caught and corrected, and campaigns only spin up for real, actionable events.
