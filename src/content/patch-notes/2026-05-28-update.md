A daily news briefing you can listen to, and a nationwide view alongside your own state.

## ✨ New features

- **A daily news briefing,** delivered as a push and as a section in your brief, with a matching in-app notification so nothing is lost if you miss the alert. ([30e8666](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/30e86664239841a416518da7fddeff78f09211a2), [0798987](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/0798987d6459534e3b2f4622519809b50751468b))
- **"Across the country"** — what's moving in other states, next to what's moving in yours. ([47b55eb](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/47b55eb705bcdd4c48eb5310a475bf7c499ded22))
- **Alerts are clickable, and you can listen to them.** First step toward every long-form page being playable. ([f0fb637](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/f0fb63780200f7278db20c574aa064bdc9fc52f9))

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Push notifications land on the real site.** Links built on a developer machine could point at localhost; they are now always absolute production URLs. ([c81c678](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/c81c6788d0ac38ea0768ebd4288877946c41306f), [ab5746d](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/ab5746d002ef4c2edff7dd1ac7f19604752facda))
- **The staleness monitor actually covers every job** — the registry was missing entries and one check had a bug that stopped it early. ([c5f1dfb](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/c5f1dfb19a7da8d595b6998676fd76dc03285b0e), [59d9d54](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/59d9d54e2b8d1ac1a195c092cef9f34afe232ae8), [849bce3](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/849bce39f3b8bbadb75b1aa90e89a22d285b4f98))
- **The daily brief is on by default,** and legislator data syncs daily. ([c42bd45](https://github.com/ohjustadam/ikratom/commit/c42bd45cf1642a40941748815e3ca2d1bddc537f))

## 🛠 Under the hood

Reliability, security and maintenance work landed this period too. Nothing you need to do — the toolbelt just gets sturdier.
