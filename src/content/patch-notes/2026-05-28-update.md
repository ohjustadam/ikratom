A daily news briefing you can listen to, and a nationwide view alongside your own state.

## ✨ New features

- **A daily news briefing,** delivered as a push and as a section in your brief, with a matching in-app notification so nothing is lost if you miss the alert.
- **"Across the country"** — what's moving in other states, next to what's moving in yours.
- **Alerts are clickable, and you can listen to them.** First step toward every long-form page being playable.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Push notifications open the right page.** Tapping one always takes you to the live site.
- **The daily brief is on by default,** and legislator data syncs daily.
