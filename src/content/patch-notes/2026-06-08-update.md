## ✨ New features

- **A "protection incoming" map color** for laws approved but not yet in force, with each state's reason on hover.
- **Share to iKratom** from your phone's share menu, and the tip form fills itself in.
- **A more active community view**, with a Lounge popup, an activity map and a searchable list of states.
- **Local meetings we find automatically** now appear on the events page.

## 🐛 Fixes

- **Email connection.** If your connection has expired, we now say so and ask you to reconnect.
- **Failed sends show the real reason.**
